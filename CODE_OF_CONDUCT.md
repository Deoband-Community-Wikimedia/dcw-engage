# Code of Conduct
**DCW Engage Portal** · *Deoband Community Wikimedia (DCW)*

---

## 1. Purpose

**DCW Engage** is the platform where community members apply for scholarships, fellowships, internships, volunteer roles, and other opportunities offered by **Deoband Community Wikimedia (DCW)**. It is also an open-source software project built and maintained by community contributors from diverse regions and backgrounds.

Both applicants and contributors deserve a safe, respectful, and welcoming environment:
* **Applicants** share personal information and often interact from a vulnerable position.
* **Contributors** freely dedicate their time, effort, and technical expertise.

This Code of Conduct outlines our standards for community interaction. As a Wikimedia affiliate, DCW strictly adheres to the [Wikimedia Universal Code of Conduct (UCoC)](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Universal_Code_of_Conduct) and the [Wikimedia Technical Spaces Code of Conduct](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Code_of_Conduct). This document adapts those global principles specifically for DCW Engage. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency, the **Wikimedia UCoC shall prevail**.

---

## 2. Scope & Applicability

This Code applies across all spaces associated with DCW Engage, including but not limited to:

* **GitHub Repository:** Issues, Pull Requests (PRs), code reviews, discussions, commit messages, and project boards.
* **DCW Engage Portal:** Application forms, free-text responses, uploaded documents, and messages sent through the platform.
* **Organiser Workspace:** How organisers review, evaluate, score, comment on, and communicate regarding applications.
* **Communication Channels:** Official emails, chat channels, video meetings, and in-person events organised for DCW Engage.
* **Off-Platform Conduct:** Any conduct outside these spaces that impacts a community member in connection with DCW Engage (e.g., harassment of an applicant or contributor on external platforms).

**Who must follow this code:**  
Applicants, organisers, repository owners, maintainers, code reviewers, contributors, and visitors.

## 3. Our Community Standards

### 3.1 Expected Behaviour

* **Civility & Respect:** Critique ideas, not individuals. Assume good faith, exercise patience, and ask clarifying questions when unsure.
* **Inclusivity:** Welcome individuals from all backgrounds, regions, and skill levels. Many community members are non-native English speakers—please communicate clearly and show patience.
* **Constructive Feedback:** Provide specific, helpful, and kind feedback. Receive feedback graciously.
* **Honesty & Transparency:** Applicants must provide truthful and accurate information. Contributors and organisers must remain open about their capacity, constraints, and mistakes.
* **Accountability:** Take responsibility for mistakes, offer sincere apologies, and learn from constructive feedback.
* **Privacy First:** Respect personal data and handle others' information with the same care you expect for your own (see [Section 5](#5-privacy--data-protection)).
* **Collaboration:** Give due credit for others' work and actively support newcomers.

### 3.2 Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are strictly prohibited:

* **Harassment:** Insults, personal attacks, intimidation, stalking, repeated unwanted contact, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
* **Discrimination:** Demeaning, offensive, or derogatory language based on age, caste, class, colour, culture, disability, educational background, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, language/accent/fluency, nationality, neurodiversity, political or religious beliefs, race, sexual orientation, or socio-economic status.
* **Sexual Harassment:** Unwelcome sexual attention, suggestive comments, or inappropriate sexual content.
* **Threats & Harm:** Threats of violence, incitement of harm, or encouraging self-harm.
* **Doxxing:** Unauthorised sharing of an individual's personal or sensitive information (e.g., real-world identity, contact details, physical location, or application contents).
* **Abuse of Power:** Pressuring, retaliating against, or showing undue favouritism toward individuals based on authority, status, or privilege.
* **Deception & Fraud:** Falsifying identity, fabricating application details or supporting documents, sockpuppeting to manipulate outcomes, spamming, or submitting misleading contributions.
* **Technical Space Abuse:** Submitting automated/spam PRs, flooding issue trackers, or deliberately ignoring maintainers' administrative decisions.
* **Inappropriate Content:** Sharing or soliciting illegal, sexually explicit, or intentionally shocking material.

## 4. Additional Commitments for Organisers & Maintainers

Organisers, maintainers, and repository owners who hold administrative access or decision-making power carry extra responsibilities:

1. **Fair Evaluation:** Judge applicants strictly on established eligibility criteria—never on personal acquaintance, region, caste, religion, gender, or background.
2. **Conflict of Interest (CoI):** Disclose any personal relation or conflict of interest regarding an applicant immediately, and recuse yourself from reviewing or deciding on that application.
3. **Courteous Communication:** Keep rejection notes and feedback respectful, professional, and constructive.
4. **Strict Confidentiality:** Do not share applicant scores, internal review comments, or submitted materials outside the authorised review panel.
5. **Proper Use of Access:** System access and credentials must be used solely for DCW work. Exporting, copying, or using applicant data for personal, commercial, or non-DCW purposes is strictly forbidden.
6. **No Exploitation:** Do not seek personal favours, monetary benefits, gifts, or inappropriate personal contact from applicants or contributors.
7. **Proactive Reporting:** Immediately report any observed Code of Conduct violations or data security lapses.

## 5. Privacy & Data Protection

The DCW Engage portal processes sensitive personal data. Protecting this data is both a technical requirement and a conduct obligation.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Never commit personal or sensitive data to the public repository.**

* **Restricted Access:** Do not access, copy, or distribute applicant data unless authorised and required for your designated role.
* **Clean Code & Commits:** Never commit live applicant data, passwords, API tokens, `.env` files, `config.php`, or production logs to public issues, PRs, or repository branches. Always use dummy/mock data in bug reports and screenshots.
* **Development Safety:** Use synthetic test datasets during development and local testing.
* **Vulnerability Reporting:** Follow the project's `SECURITY.md` guidelines. Report security bugs privately—**never open a public GitHub Issue for security vulnerabilities**.
* **Data Retention:** Adhere to portal data scrubbing and retention schedules for inactive or rejected application records.

## 6. Contributing Responsibly

* **Code vs. Person:** Keep code review comments objective and focused on the code, not the author.
* **Maintainer Discretion:** Maintainers retain the right to decline or close PRs that do not align with project goals or quality standards. Respect maintainer decisions.
* **Licensing & Rights:** Ensure all submitted code and materials comply with the project’s **MIT License**. Clearly declare any third-party code or AI-generated material included in your contributions.

## 7. Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it immediately through our contact channels. 

### How to Report
* **Email:** [`moderator@dcwwiki.org`](mailto:moderator@dcwwiki.org) *(DCW Moderators)*
* **GitHub:** Use private reporting mechanisms or contact a maintainer directly via email/private channel for repository-related issues.
* **Emergencies:** If there is an immediate physical safety risk, contact your local emergency services first.

### What to Include in Your Report
To help us investigate effectively, please provide:
* Details of the incident (what happened, date, time, and location/link).
* Names or GitHub usernames of the individuals involved.
* Supporting evidence (e.g., screenshots or context), ensuring non-involved private details are redacted.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not raise conduct complaints in public GitHub Issues or Pull Request discussions.** Public calls-out can escalate harm and expose personal data. Always report privately.

### Confidentiality & Anti-Retaliation
* **Confidentiality:** Reports will be kept confidential and shared strictly on a need-to-know basis.
* **Protection Against Retaliation:** Retaliation against anyone reporting a concern in good faith is a severe violation and will result in direct disciplinary action.
* **Bad-Faith Reports:** Deliberately false or malicious reports made to harm another member are prohibited and treated as conduct violations.

## 8. Enforcement & Response

### 8.1 Incident Response Team
Reports are reviewed by the **DCW Moderators**, and the Board. Members with a conflict of interest in a case will recuse themselves.

### 8.2 Response Workflow
| Step | Action | Timeline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Acknowledgment** | Confirm receipt of the report to the reporter. | Within **3 working days** |
| **2. Investigation** | Review evidence and hear perspectives from relevant parties fairly. | Case dependent |
| **3. Decision** | Determine appropriate corrective action and notify involved parties. | Promptly upon review |
| **4. Follow-up** | Monitor the situation to prevent repeat occurrences or retaliation. | Ongoing |

### 8.3 Enforcement Outcomes
Depending on the severity and history of the violation, actions may include:

1. **Private Caution:** Clarification of expectations or a formal private reminder.
2. **Warning & Apology:** Public or private warning requiring a formal apology or correction of conduct.
3. **Temporary Restrictions:** Temporary block from repository activity (issues, PRs, discussions) or portal access.
4. **Content Removal:** Deletion of violating comments, issues, or code contributions.
5. **Access Revocation:** Removal of organiser, maintainer, or administrative privileges and invalidation of access tokens.
6. **Permanent Exclusion:** Permanent ban from all DCW Engage platforms, repositories, and workspaces.
7. **Application Disqualification:** Cancellation of candidacy or withdrawal of an opportunity offer if fraud or manipulation is detected.
8. **Legal Escalation:** Referral to legal authorities or relevant Wikimedia bodies where required.

*Note: Reporting a concern will never negatively impact an applicant's candidacy or standing.*

### 8.4 Immediate Safety Intervention
Maintainers and workspace owners reserve the right to take immediate action to prevent ongoing harm (e.g., hiding abusive comments, locking issue threads, or temporarily revoking access) while a formal review is conducted.

---

## 9. Escalation & Appeals

* **Appeals Process:** If you believe an enforcement decision was made unfairly or based on incorrect facts, you may request an appeal within **30 days** by emailing [`dcw@wikimedia.org`](mailto:dcw@wikimedia.org). An independent reviewer non-involved in the original decision will evaluate the appeal.
* **Wikimedia Escalation:** For matters occurring in broader Wikimedia technical or community spaces, or where internal resolution is unsuitable due to conflicts of interest, reports may be escalated to the [Wikimedia Universal Code of Conduct Coordinating Committee (U4C)](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Universal_Code_of_Conduct/Coordinating_Committee) or the Wikimedia Technical Code of Conduct Committee.

## 10. Language & Accessibility

DCW supports a multilingual community. You may submit reports, appeals, or queries in **English, Hindi, or Urdu**. We will make every effort to communicate and respond in your preferred language.

* Approved translations of this Code will be linked in this repository as they become available.
* In the case of any interpretation ambiguity or conflict across translations, the **English version shall serve as the official reference text**.

*If you require accessibility accommodations or time-zone adjustments to participate in our processes, please inform us so we can support you.*
